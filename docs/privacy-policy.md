**Effective date:** November 27, 2026

SaffBuddy helps Ohio State students find people on campus to pray in congregation with. It is run by Yousif Khalid Abdou, an individual developer ("SaffBuddy", "we", "us"), and is not affiliated with The Ohio State University. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, how long it's kept, and how to delete it. It applies to the SaffBuddy app and the website saffbuddy.app. Using SaffBuddy is also governed by our [Terms of Use](https://saffbuddy.app/terms).

We collect as little as we can: SaffBuddy never keeps a record of who prayed where.

## What we collect

| Data | Why | How long we keep it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your OSU email address | To sign you in with a one-time code. Only @osu.edu addresses can sign up, except one account we run for Apple's app review. | Until you delete your account |
| Your first name | Shown to you on your profile | Until you delete your account |
| Your gender (brother or sister) | To keep brothers' and sisters' requests and rooms separate | Until you delete your account |
| Your current request or the request you joined (room and start time) | So others can see there is a congregation and join it | Deleted automatically about 10 minutes after the start time, or sooner if you cancel or leave |
| Sign-in records kept by our login provider (such as the time and IP address of a sign-in) | Security and abuse prevention | Kept by Supabase under its own retention rules, which may extend after your account is deleted |
| Technical logs of requests to our server (time, IP address and account ID; never which room) | Security and debugging | A few days, under Supabase's log retention |
| Live-update signals (a timestamp and the brothers' or sisters' channel; no account or room) | Telling open screens to refresh | A few days, then deleted by Supabase |
| Emails you send us, for example through **Report a problem** (your email address, what you write, and your phone's operating system and version, which the app adds to the draft so you can see it before sending) | To answer you and fix problems | As long as needed to answer you and keep a record of the conversation. You can ask us to delete it. |

We also keep **anonymous daily counts** per prayer room: how many congregations happened in a room on a day and how many people joined them. These counts contain no names, accounts, email addresses, or times of day, only the room and the date, and aren't linked to any account.

If you've chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us crash reports for SaffBuddy. Apple decides what these contain; they don't include your name or email address.

On your phone, the app keeps your sign-in so you stay logged in. It's removed when you sign out or delete your account.

## What we don't collect

- **Your location.** If you allow it, the map shows where you are. Your location stays on your phone: SaffBuddy uses it only to move the map, and never sends it to our servers, saves it, or logs it. The map itself is provided by Apple Maps (or Google Maps on Android) under their own privacy policies. You can turn location off at any time in your phone's settings, and the app still works.
- **Prayer or location history.** Once a request expires, it's deleted. We don't record which rooms you went to or when.
- **Contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.** The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools of its own, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

## Who can see your information

- **Other users never see your name or email.** A request shows only the room, the start time, and how many people have joined. Requests from the other gender aren't shown to you, and yours aren't shown to them.
- **Our service providers** handle data only to run SaffBuddy:
  - [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) hosts the database and handles sign-in. Data is stored in the United States (US East).
  - [Resend](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy) sends the sign-in code to your email address.
  - Apple Maps (and Google Maps on Android) show the campus map. If you tap Directions, the room's location opens in Apple Maps or Google Maps, which use your location to plan the route under their own privacy policies.
  - Cloudflare forwards emails sent to support@saffbuddy.app to our inbox.
- **We don't sell or rent your information, share it for advertising, or give it to anyone else**, except as described here.
- **Legal reasons.** We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that it's required by law, a court order, or other legal process, or that it's necessary to protect someone from serious harm. Because of how SaffBuddy is built, we don't have location or prayer history to disclose.
- **If SaffBuddy changes hands.** If someone else takes over running SaffBuddy, your information may move with it, and this policy will keep applying to it unless you're told otherwise and given the chance to delete your account.

The database itself enforces who can read which rows and columns, so these rules don't depend on the app behaving correctly.

## Security

We protect your information with access rules enforced by the database and with encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the app and our servers. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, so we can't guarantee that your information will never be accessed, disclosed, changed, or lost through a breach of our or our providers' safeguards. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

## Deleting your account

In the app, open the menu, then **Profile → Delete account**. This immediately deletes your account, name, gender, email, and any request you're in. Sign-in records held by Supabase and copies in backups may remain for a limited time before they're removed under those providers' policies. The anonymous daily counts stay, because they don't contain anything about you.

You can also email support@saffbuddy.app from your account's email address to ask for deletion, for a copy of your data, or for a correction. We'll respond within 30 days.

## Children

SaffBuddy is meant for Ohio State students and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have, we'll delete it. Users under 18 need permission from a parent or legal guardian (see our Terms of Use).

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date at the top. If a change significantly affects how your information is used, we'll make reasonable efforts to let you know in the app or by email before it takes effect.
